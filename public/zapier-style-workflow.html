Evidence-based escrow automation, milestone FSM progression, cryptographic SHA-256 verification, and decentralized dispute arbitration

START: Client needs project completed
Project Initialization
Client posts project with milestone budget
Defines clear milestones, deliverables, deadlines, and allocated budget
Freelancer Bidding
Freelancers submit proposals & technical bids
Includes cover letter, rate estimate, timeframe, and portfolio links
AI Matching Engine
AI Matcher ranks candidate bids
Evaluates skill overlap, rating history, and relevance scores
Proposal Acceptance
Client accepts top proposal & deposits escrow
Contract generated; initial milestone deposit transferred from client wallet
Engine 01 (OS FSM Engine)
Escrow funds locked atomically
State: FUNDED → IN_PROGRESS. Funds held safely in platform vault
Milestone Execution
Freelancer works & uploads deliverable
Code, design files, or report submitted before milestone deadline
Engine 02 (DSA & SE Engine)
SHA-256 cryptographic checksum computed
Deliverable hashed into tamper-evident Merkle Evidence Tree; State: UNDER_REVIEW
Client reviews deliverable within 7 days
✓ Work approved
Client Action
Client approves deliverable
Manual sign-off affirming all criteria met
State: RELEASED
Escrow released to Freelancer
Funds transferred instantly to Freelancer wallet
⏰ Client inactive for 7 days
Scheduler Daemon
Watchdog triggers auto-timeout
Engine 01 CRON verifies elapsed grace period without response
State: AUTO_RELEASED
Escrow auto-released to Freelancer
Protects freelancers against non-responsive clients
⚠ Defective work / dispute
State: DISPUTED
Dispute raised & escrow frozen
Funds locked; mutual evidence submission period opens
Arbitration Panel
Arbitrator inspects Merkle Evidence Tree
Validates SHA-256 hashes against original contract scope
Ruling in favor of?
Client
Full Refund
Freelancer
Funds Released
Are more milestones remaining?
↺ Yes: Next milestone starts (Loops back to Escrow Funds Locked)
↓ No: All milestones complete (Proceeds to Final Settlement)
Engine 03 (DBMS Engine)
Audit trail chained into immutable ledger
Relational BCNF schema stores append-only transaction history & Merkle root signatures
END: Contract completed & funds settled
