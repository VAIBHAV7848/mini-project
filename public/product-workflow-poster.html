STEP 1
📋
Project & Milestones
Client creates a structured project listing:
  • Defines Scope & Acceptance Criteria
  • Configures Sequential Milestones
  • Sets Milestone Budgets & Due Dates
  • Specifies required skill tags
Initiated by Client
STEP 2
🎯
Bidding & AI Match
Freelancers bid and get scored deterministically:
  • Freelancers submit milestone bids & cover notes
  • AI Matcher ranks proposals (0-100%):
  • 50% Skill Jaccard + 30% Budget + 20% DevScore
  • Client reviews top-ranked bids
Algorithmic Ranking
STEP 3
🔒
Atomic Escrow Deposit
Client accepts bid and locks milestone capital:
  • Client funds escrow via UPI QR or Wallet
  • KeyedMutex acquires concurrency lock
  • ACID ledger debits client & credits escrow
  • Status changes: AWAITING_DEPOSIT ➔ FUNDED
Guaranteed Funds (Zero Counterparty Risk)
STEP 4
💻
Execution & SHA-256
Freelancer completes work and delivers proof:
  • Transitions to IN_PROGRESS
  • Freelancer uploads deliverable archive (ZIP/Code)
  • Streaming SHA-256 Checksum computed
  • Enters UNDER_REVIEW (starts 7-day clock)
Cryptographic Fingerprint Generated
✅ Path A: Client Approval (Happy Path)
Client inspects work in Deliverable Inspector, verifies SHA-256 checksum, and approves:

• Escrow ledger executes instant double-entry transfer to Freelancer's wallet.
• Milestone transitions to terminal state: RELEASED.
• Next sequential milestone automatically unlocks and is ready for funding!
⏱️ Path B: 7-Day Watchdog Timeout
If the Client becomes unresponsive / ghosts for 7 full calendar days after delivery:

• Automated Review Watchdog Scheduler triggers.
• Transitions milestone: UNDER_REVIEW ➔ REVIEW_TIMEOUT.
• Auto-disburses escrow to the Freelancer, preventing developer starvation!
⚖️ Path C: Evidence Dispute Tribunal
If work is defective or disputed by either party:

• Escrow is immediately frozen in DISPUTED state.
• Counter-evidence & test logs form an N-ary Merkle Evidence Tree.
• Impartial Dispute Reviewer renders binding ruling (Release / Refund / Split).
Core Invariant: Conservation of Funds (Δ Client + Δ Escrow + Δ Freelancer = 0) · Every transition linked in an append-only SHA-256 Audit Log.
